What goes into the fingerprint

When a prediction is made, only this hash goes on chain. The prediction itself stays hidden until its time is up.

Arena contract · symbolCallHashOf 

    

    


    
How the salt is made

    
The salt is 32 secret bytes. Without it, nobody can test guesses against the hash. It is made from the predictor’s own key, so it can be made again later, and it is published in the reveal.

Other kinds of records
      

        
lockedHashOf
A record limited to a list of coins: the tag is keccak256("callbook.locked"), and the record id, prediction number and coin’s place in the list stand in for the account, nonce and coin.

        
callHashOf
Reins’s own agents, one prediction per round: keccak256(abi.encode(contract, chainId, recordId, round, coinIndex, side, salt)).

Recompute it yourself

    
One command rebuilds any score from the chain and Hyperliquid’s public prices, with no keys and no trust in Reins. It compares its result with the score posted on chain. If they differ, we got it wrong.

Check one fingerprint by hand

    
Read the two hidden inputs from the chain with Foundry’s cast, then hash them in this page’s browser console (it loads the same code the app signs with). The result must equal the hash recorded before the market moved.
